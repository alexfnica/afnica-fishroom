# 🐟 AFNICA Aquarium

A relaxing aquarium simulator - feed, breed, and grow your collection of fish, keep your tanks healthy, and build out your own underwater world.

> **Status: early prototype, actively in development.** Core systems work, but a lot is still being tuned, cleaned up, and rebalanced. Expect rough edges.

## What's in it

- **Multiple species** with real-life-inspired care needs, including Corydoras (with distinct life stages) and Bristlenose Pleco breeding
- **Genetics-based breeding** - offspring traits depend on the parent fish
- **Health & disease system** - fish can get sick and need treatment
- **Water quality simulation** - bioload, filtration, and tank upkeep affect your fish
- **Multiple tanks** to manage and grow into
- **Tank decorating** and an in-game economy (buy, sell, upgrade)

## Tech

A single self-contained HTML file - no build step, no server, no external dependencies. Everything (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and all art assets) lives in one file and runs entirely in the browser. Progress is saved locally via `localStorage`.

## How to play

Download `AFNICA_Aquarium_V17_40_Bristlenose_Breeding.html` and open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Works offline, no installation needed.
